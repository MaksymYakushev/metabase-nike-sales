# Nike Store Analysis 

An end-to-end data analytics project based on Nike sales transactions in the United States.
The project focuses on data exploration, SQL analysis, KPI development, and interactive dashboarding in Metabase.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Nike sales data covering 9,360 transactions across different U.S. regions from **January 1, 2020**, to **December 31, 2021**.

<img src="https://github.com/MaksymYakushev/metabase-nike-sales/blob/main/images/project-preview/preview.gif" width="900" height="650">


## **Business Context**

Nike operates across multiple regions, retailers, products, and sales channels, making it important to understand where revenue is generated and how sales performance changes over time.

This project uses Nike sales transaction data from the United States to provide a consolidated view of business performance. The analysis focuses on identifying sales trends, understanding product and regional contribution, and comparing key business metrics across different dimensions.

The analysis covers **9,360 transactions** recorded between **January 1, 2020, and December 31, 2021**, representing **48 states, 6 retailers, and multiple Nike product categories**.

## **Business Value**

The dashboard is designed to support data-driven analysis of Nike's sales performance by providing a centralized view of the most important sales metrics.

It helps users to:

- Monitor overall revenue, units sold, and transaction volume
- Track monthly revenue trends and identify changes in sales performance
- Understand revenue contribution across different Nike products
- Compare revenue distribution across U.S. regions
- Evaluate average transaction value and average price per unit
- Understand the scale of the business across products, retailers, and states
- Identify areas that may require further investigation through more detailed analysis

## **Dashboard Features**

The interactive Metabase dashboard provides a centralized view of Nike's sales performance and allows users to explore the business across several key dimensions.

Key features include:

- **KPI Overview** — provides a high-level view of revenue, units sold, transactions, average transaction value, and other core business metrics.
- **Sales Trend Monitoring** — tracks revenue performance over time and highlights changes in monthly sales activity.
- **Product Performance** — compares products based on revenue contribution and overall sales performance.
- **Regional Analysis** — shows how sales are distributed across U.S. regions and helps identify differences in regional performance.
- **Retailer Analysis** — enables comparison of sales performance across Nike's retail partners.
- **Sales Channel Analysis** — examines performance across different sales methods, including Online, In-store, and Outlet.
- **Geographic Analysis** — provides a more detailed view of sales performance at the state level.
- **Interactive Filtering** — allows users to dynamically explore the data and focus on specific products, regions, retailers, sales methods, and time periods.
